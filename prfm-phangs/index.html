Comparison with Observations of Nearby Galaxies (PHANGS)¶

Introduction¶

Here, we utilize the PHANGS data release to recompute the gas weight and reproduce Figure 4 of Sun et al. 2023.

  • Paper: https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ApJ...945L..19S

  • Code: https://github.com/PhangsTeam/MegaTable

  • Data: https://www.canfar.net/storage/vault/list/phangs/RELEASES/Sun_etal_2022

Data Loading¶

We use the hexagon aperture (1.5 kpc) from the PHANGS megatable v4.0 (Sun et al. 2022). compute_prfm_inputs adds the derived columns Sigma_gas, total orbital frequency Omega, H_star, and their uncertainties.

PRFM Predictions¶

Apply the self-consistent PRFM solver to every valid row. The PHANGS rotation curve constrains the total orbital frequency Ω=Vcirc/R\Omega=V_\mathrm{circ}/RΩ=Vcirc​/R, not the dark-matter-only frequency Ωd\Omega_dΩd​. The baseline therefore includes gas and stellar gravity but omits the halo term. The solver returns:

  • P_weight — dynamical equilibrium pressure PDE/kBP_\mathrm{DE}/k_BPDE​/kB​ [K cm−3^{-3}−3]

  • H_gas — self-consistent gas scale height [pc]

  • sigma_eff_sol — self-consistent effective velocity dispersion [km s−1^{-1}−1]

  • Sigma_SFR_pred — PRFM-predicted SFR surface density [M⊙M_\odotM⊙​ yr−1^{-1}−1 kpc−2^{-2}−2]

import warnings
warnings.filterwarnings("ignore")

# Baseline: gas and stellar gravity, with no halo term.
t_pred = phangs.run_prfm(t)

# Valid rows for original data
cols=["Sigma_gas","Sigma_atom","Sigma_mol","Sigma_star",
      "Sigma_SFR_HaW4recal","Sigma_SFR_FUVW4recal","Sigma_SFR_Hacorr"]
for c_ in cols:
    data_mask = phangs.valid_rows(
        t_pred, cols=[c_]
    )
    print(f"Valid data rows for {c_}: {data_mask.sum()} / {len(t_pred)}")
data_mask = phangs.valid_rows(t_pred, cols=cols, rel_error=0.1)
print(f"Valid data rows for all: {data_mask.sum()} / {len(t_pred)}")

# Valid rows for PRFM output
prfm_mask = phangs.valid_rows(

t_clean = t_pred[prfm_mask]

Upper-bound test: treating total Ω\OmegaΩ as a halo frequency¶

The megatable value Ω=Vcirc/R\Omega=V_\mathrm{circ}/RΩ=Vcirc​/R is generated by the total enclosed mass. A physical halo term requires a halo-only mass model. In this section only, omega_d_col="Omega" deliberately sets Ωd=Ω\Omega_d=\OmegaΩd​=Ω. This attributes all circular support to dark matter while gas and stellar gravity are already included, so it is an upper bound rather than a physical decomposition.
