\subsection{Sampling Diagnostics}
\label{sec:sampling_diagnostics}

We apply the KDE-Sobol design procedure of \autoref{sec:sampling} to
three target gas surface densities, $\Sgas^{\rm tgt} \in \{5, 10,
20\}\Surf$, each with a band half-width $\Delta = 0.3\dex$.
The resulting reference sets contain 385, 791, and 899 \PHANGS\
apertures respectively, drawn from the full 80-galaxy sample. The
marginal distributions of the five design fields for the initial
$n = 32$ suite, compared to the \PHANGS\ reference set at
$\Sgas^{\rm tgt} = 10\Surf$, are shown in
\autoref{fig:field_distributions}.

\autoref{tab:fairness} quantifies sampling fairness via the per-field
quantile mismatch $\varepsilon_a$ (\autoref{eq:eps_a}) for
$\Sgas^{\rm tgt} = 10\Surf$ at three sample sizes. All values satisfy
the $\varepsilon_{\rm tol} = 0.10\dex$ criterion: the worst-case mismatch
across all five fields and all sample sizes is $0.054\dex$, achieved for
$\Sstar$ at $n = 64$. The fairness is stable across sample sizes: doubling
$n$ from 64 to 128 or 256 does not meaningfully reduce $\varepsilon$,
indicating that the dominant source of mismatch is the KDE bandwidth
smoothing the observed distribution, not finite Sobol sample size.
Results for $\Sgas^{\rm tgt} \in \{5, 20\}\Surf$ are qualitatively
similar, with $\varepsilon_{\rm env} \leq 0.07\dex$ in all cases
(\autoref{fig:design_fields}).
