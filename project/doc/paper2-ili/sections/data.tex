\section{Observational Data}
\label{sec:data}

The observational constraints for the inference framework are drawn from the same
\PHANGS\ megatable dataset described in Paper~I \citep[Kim et al., in prep.;][]{2022AJ....164...43S,2023ApJ...945L..19S}. We refer the reader to Paper~I for
details of the data reduction, aperture definitions, and derived quantities.

For the inference problem, the relevant observables at each aperture are:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{x}_{\rm obs} = (\Sgas,\, \Ssfr,\, \fmol,\, \PDE,\, \seff)
\end{equation}
where $\Ssfr$ is the star formation rate surface density, $\fmol = \Smol/\Sgas$ is
the molecular fraction, $\PDE$ is the dynamical equilibrium pressure, and $\seff$
is the effective ISM velocity dispersion. [SKELETON --- summary statistics and
completeness masks to be documented here]
